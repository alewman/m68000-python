# m68000-python 0.1.0

The first release: a readable, pure-Python MC68000 instruction core in the
shape of z80-python, verified up a ladder of oracles ordered by tier, with
a claim boundary for what no available evidence settles.

## What it is

- The whole 68000 instruction set: all 45,815 defined first words, the
  two-word prefetch queue and the bus sequence of every instruction, the
  clock total of every step and the clock at which each access ends
  (`step_clocks`), the exception model with its two frame shapes, the
  interrupt lifecycle with the acknowledge cycle and the E-clock wait, trace,
  STOP and RESET.
- The embedding contract of the family: four bus callables over a 24-bit
  space, `step()` returning clocks, the host deciding everything else
  (`acknowledge`, `tas_write`, `address_error`, `reset_devices` and
  `function_codes` as optional keywords; `BusError` to assert BERR).
- Tooling in z80-python's shape: `CPUState`, a disassembler in MAME's
  spelling, `DebugSession` with breakpoints, watchpoints and bus-access
  tracking, versioned JSON Lines traces with first-divergence comparison,
  `CommandDebugger` and `python -m m68000_python`.

## What it claims, and how strongly

Every behaviour carries a status in [docs/claims.md](../claims.md):

- **Verified** (hardware-captured): `ABCD`, `SBCD` and `NBCD`, every input
  and every flag, against flamewing's Genesis-captured tables.
- **Strong** (a hardware-corrected emulator run inside its checked scope,
  plus another lineage): instruction results and defined flags for every
  defined first word; the undefined flags of DIV and CHK; the decoder;
  group 1 and 2 exception entry; an address error's clocks and most of its
  frame; `LINK A7`; `ADDQ.W #,An` at 8 clocks.
- **Provisional** (MAME's lineage, or the manual): exact clock totals, the
  order of bus cycles, `step_clocks`, trace, interrupts and STOP.
- **Contested** (credible sources disagree; the core follows the gate): six
  address-error behaviours, listed with the gate cases they touch.
- **Undecidable here**: three 2-clock questions, the reset exception's
  total and the double bus fault.
- **Outside the contract**: the contents of a bus-error frame, timing
  inside an instruction, the 68010 and later, the 68008's timing.

The evidence: the SingleStepTests/m68000 gate, 317,500 cases with every bus
access compared and no case excluded; WinUAE's CPU-tester core and Musashi
built from pinned sources and run over the gate; the MAME 0.285 lockstep on
52.8 million instructions of Altered Beast on two boards; the 680x0 corpus
as a classified detector; a coverage map over every defined word and 176
seeded mutants, 174 killed and the two survivors equivalent. The record with
every pin, command and timing is [docs/validation.md](../validation.md).

## Readability and sources

Every handler's docstring starts with its Motorola name and ends with the
manual page or table its rule comes from; every handler that encodes a rule
the manuals do not give names the corpus file, the WinUAE run or the
hardware tables that pin it. `tests/test_readability.py` enforces all of it.
`docs/start-here.md` maps each section of the primer to the module that
implements it.

## Compatibility

Nothing was released before 0.1.0. Two changes relative to the development
tree the family's boards built against are listed under **Breaking** in
[CHANGELOG.md](../../CHANGELOG.md): console numbers are decimal or prefixed
hexadecimal, and `reset_devices` is a constructor keyword. Pre-1.0 minor
releases may break the public surface when the changelog says so
([docs/api-stability.md](../api-stability.md)).
